About

This is Hanyu Zhu. I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at University of Massachusetts Dartmouth. I obtained my bachelor's degree from Sichuan University.

My current research interests mainly lie in (1) security and privacy: attack, defense, and safety alignment for large language models; (2) trustworthy AI systems: security evaluation and robustness enhancement of large-model-enabled systems (e.g., Vision-Language-Action models) in real-world physical interactions and wireless sensing scenarios.
